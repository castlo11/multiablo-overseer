# MultiAblo — Orchestrated Realms for Diablo 2 Resurrected

Welcome to **MultiAblo**, a companion toolkit designed for players who crave the symphony of simultaneous journeys through the Burning Hells. Instead of a single hero facing the darkness alone, imagine a coordinated ensemble of characters—each traversing their own Sanctuary, yet moving in perfect harmony under your command. This project is not merely a launcher; it is a conductor's baton for the modern Diablo 2 Resurrected experience.

## Why MultiAblo Exists

In the world of action RPGs, the ability to manage multiple game instances opens a doorway to efficiency—whether you are orchestrating a self-found hardcore challenge, managing a dedicated magic-find circuit, or simply desiring a smoother way to transfer items between your own characters without third-party intermediaries. Traditional multi-boxing solutions often feel clunky, akin to juggling with boxing gloves. MultiAblo was born from a desire for **graceful orchestration**, offering a refined, responsive, and visually coherent interface that respects both your desktop space and your mental bandwidth.

This tool focuses on the **art of presence**. It flickers between your active realms with a tactile smoothness, reducing the cognitive load of switching contexts. The core philosophy here is *unity through multiplicity*—your game windows become a single, fluid workspace.

## 🛡️ Core Capabilities & Features

MultiAblo is not just a window manager; it's a comprehensive companion for the dedicated player. Explore the facets that make it an indispensable part of your journey.

### 🌀 Seamless Instance Orchestration
- **Profile-Based Launch Sequences**: Define specific characters or build configurations, and launch them in a single keystroke. Each profile preserves resolution, audio settings, and graphics preferences, ensuring that your Paladin's realm runs at maximum fidelity while your Sorceress's window remains lightweight for rapid load times.
- **Smart Focus Switching**: The interface intuitively follows your cursor, allowing you to move between game windows without alt-tabbing through a clunky OS menu. This feels less like task-switching and more like walking through a door into another room.

### 🧠 Intelligent Resource Allocation
- **Adaptive Priority Engine**: The background instances are gently throttled to reduce CPU and GPU strain, allowing the active window to shine. This isn't a blunt force cap; it's a dynamic balance that learns your play patterns, ensuring the foreground realm always receives the computational love it deserves.
- **Memory-Friendly Architecture**: Designed to be a lightweight shim, MultiAblo maintains a minimal memory footprint, ensuring its own presence doesn't interfere with the memory-hungry demands of Diablo 2 Resurrected's modern 3D engine.

### 🎨 Luxurious Interface & Accessibility
- **Responsive Command Palette**: A sleek, searchable palette lets you issue commands—like "swap to character B" or "screenshot all"—without memorizing hotkeys. This interface is fully localizable, supporting multiple languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Korean, and Traditional Chinese.
- **Dark-Themed Cohesion**: The UI adopts a gothic, minimalist aesthetic that complements D2R's dark fantasy palette, reducing eye strain during extended grind sessions. The layout is configurable, allowing you to dock control panels or let them auto-hide.

### ⚡ Synchronization Utilities
- **Cross-Instance Clipboard**: Copy text (like a trade message or a map note) in one instance and paste it into another with a dedicated hotkey, bypassing the OS clipboard restrictions that often block game windows.
- **Global Pause/Resume**: Send a universal "pause" command to all background instances when you need a moment to breathe, preventing unwanted monster encounters while you strategize.

## 🧩 Getting Started

Embarking on your orchestrated journey is simple and intuitive. This section guides you through the initial setup and configuration, ensuring you spend more time slaying demons and less time fiddling with menus.

### Prerequisites for Your Symphony
- A valid installation of Diablo 2 Resurrected.
- A 64-bit operating system (Windows 10 or 11 recommended).
- A minimum of 8GB RAM; 16GB or more is recommended for a comfortable multi-instance experience.

### Initial Configuration
1. **First Launch**: Upon running MultiAblo, it will automatically detect your D2R installation directory.
2. **Character Profiles**: Create your first profile by navigating to the "Ensemble" section. Add your game accounts and select the character names you wish to orchestrate.
3. **Bind Your Controls**: The "Conducting" tab allows you to assign global hotkeys for window cycling, quick-swapping, and the clipboard utility. The default bindings are intuitive, but the system encourages personalization.

## 💎 Advanced Usage

For the veteran conductor, MultiAblo offers deeper layers of control.

### Custom Scripts & Macros
The *Lore Weaver* module allows you to create simple automation routines. For example, you can script a sequence that launches your Barbarian, waits for the game to load, then switches focus to your Enchantress to buff him, all while keeping your hands free for your beverage of choice. These scripts are written in a simple, human-readable syntax that mimics natural language.

### Real-Time Telemetry
The *HUD of the Watcher* provides a non-intrusive overlay on your main monitor, displaying CPU usage per instance, current FPS, and an estimated item filter status. This telemetry is crucial for identifying bottlenecks in your multi-boxing setup.
